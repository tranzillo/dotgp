## The first milestone (readiness: drivers against their own teachers)

- 2026-10-23 **readiness: oval stable v10** @8627ff6 · oval · lab — 0 of 14 oval cells ready (presets + a generated oval of each size class, 20 and 40 cars, 150 km, 4 seeds). Pace within 0.4-2.5% of teachers everywhere. Students lose 9-18 of 20 and 27-38 of 40 where teachers lose 0-2 (flat track within noise: 1.3 and 13). The teachers themselves aren't clean on bigger generated ovals: intermediate 2.5 / 10.0, speedway 0 / 8.8, superspeedway 6.5 / 18.8 (20 / 40 cars). Pit maneuver 13-49% of car-time. Table: experiments/2026-10-23-readiness-oval-stable/.
- 2026-10-23 **teachers on big ovals** @73288c2 · oval — teachers (the stable's own) lost 10-19 of 40 on generated intermediate and superspeedway ovals. Traced: a teacher committed to passing a car braking hard for pit road, stopped minding it, and barely moved across on the banking; it hit it at 77 m/s. Fix: the car being passed still limits the passer's speed until it's actually beside it. Superspeedway 13 / 10 → 5 / 7 out (two seeds), intermediate 10 / 13 → 11 / 3. What's left on the superspeedway is mostly **the pit maneuver hitting the wall** (3 in one race): for the pit skill's teacher (circuit machine). A speed-scaled lookahead (stopping distance) was tried first and changed nothing (reverted).
- 2026-10-23 **readiness: bundle v1** · both — the 40 individuals with every skill (oval skills from v10; road racing, road cautions, pits from the circuit machine): 0 of 17 cells ready, worse than v10 on ovals (16-18 of 20 out). The pit skill, trained alone, fails in pit-road traffic (short oval: ~9 of 19 retirements in pit cycles); circuits 150 km 18.9 of 20 out, sprint 5.7. The bench's scripted share counted a driver's own stops as scripted (fixed). (experiments/2026-10-23-readiness-bundle-v1)
- 2026-10-23 **pit wall nose** · both (shared teachers) — circuit teachers (stable personalities' road styles) lost 3.5 cars a race at 40 cars over 150 km; 8 of 14 were wall hits under caution at the pit wall's nose. On every track (ovals too) the nose stands 1.40 m beyond the inside edge, closer than a car's width, and the track limits allow a car's centre 1.5 m past the edge. Hitting it: the formation's "hold your lane" before the pickup (cars on the inside line), and the pit maneuver backing out of an aborted entry at 31-39 m/s. Fix (`Track.pitWallClear`): the formation, aborted entries (now at 18 m/s) and the racing-line corridor keep a car not pitting clear of the wall from 60 m before its nose. Teachers 3.5 -> 2.5 out a race, no wall hits under caution; what's left is contact as safety cars begin. Changes oval cautions too (the formation is shared). Also: the pit skill with traffic drills completes ~90% of drill stops but still loses ~6 cars a race merging on the short oval.
