Traced: a teacher committed to passing a car braking hard for pit road, stopped minding it, and barely moved across on the banking; it hit it at 77 m/s. - 2026-10-23 **readiness: bundle v1** · both — the 40 individuals with every skill (oval skills from v10; road racing, road cautions, pits from the circuit machine): 0 of 17 cells ready, worse than v10 on ovals (16-18 of 20 out). - 2026-10-23 **pit wall nose** · both (shared teachers) — circuit teachers (stable personalities' road styles) lost 3.5 cars a race at 40 cars over 150 km; 8 of 14 were wall hits under caution at the pit wall's nose. On every track (ovals too) the nose stands 1.40 m beyond the inside edge, closer than a car's width, and the track limits allow a car's centre 1.5 m past the edge. Hitting it: the formation's "hold your lane" before the pickup (cars on the inside line), and the pit maneuver backing out of an aborted entry at 31-39 m/s. Fix (`Track.pitWallClear`): the formation, aborted entries (now at 18 m/s) and the racing-line corridor keep a car not pitting clear of the wall from 60 m before its nose. Teachers 3.5 -> 2.5 out a race, no wall hits under caution; what's left is contact as safety cars begin. Changes oval cautions too (the formation is shared). Also: the pit skill with traffic drills completes ~90% of drill stops but still loses ~6 cars a race merging on the short oval.
- 2026-10-23 **why oval cars pit so much** @this commit · oval — stops by why the crew called them (new in the oval bench and circuits.ts). 40 cars, short oval, 150 km: the oval stable 532 stops a race, **100% called for damage** (tyres at 0.90 when they came in); its teachers 227, **70% for damage**, with no retirements and no cautions (damage done by fault 9.3 a race, students 26.4). - 2026-10-23 **a car a pit box** · both — circuit teachers on readiness's circuits lost 11.3 of 40 a race: under a safety car crews call cheap stops en masse, cars sharing a box queue for it, the field behind stalls on track before pit entry and race control retires it (Oxmoor: 16 boxes). - 2026-10-23 **circuit teachers: lookahead** · both (shared teachers) — after the box rule, circuit teachers on readiness's circuits lost 3.5 of 20 a race over 150 km (8 races traced). The commonest crash: a racer at 55 m/s crossing to the inside for the next corner ran into a car slowing for pit road; its lookahead (60 m, 1.1 s at that speed) saw it too late to brake. What's left: contact under caution as safety cars begin. Gate: 1 of 20.
