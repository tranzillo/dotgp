# Circuit field and its teachers in oval-like races (2026-10-23, @8cbebc7)

The human-limits circuit field (12 drivers, styled, track knowledge, PPO + ES racecraft) against its own teachers (`circuits.ts teacher:`), 6 circuits x 3 seeds, at 20 or 40 cars and 25 or 150 km. Retirements per race (students / teachers): 20/25 km 0.5 / 0.1; 40/25 km 6.2 / 0.2; 20/150 km 4.3 / 2.7; 40/150 km 14.2 / 8.6. Students' damage done by fault 0.68 / 2.51 / 3.20 / 8.61.

- **Traffic density breaks students, on circuits as on ovals.** At 25 km, doubling the field: students 0.5 -> 6.2 out (contact 1.9, barriers 1.0, beached 3.2, first lap 1.3), teachers 0.2. The near-whole copy seen at 20 cars / 25 km was the easy conditions.
- **Long races break circuit teachers too:** 2.7 out at 20 cars, 8.6 at 40 over 150 km (oval teachers ~1). Students' track-limit offences 188-297 a race against teachers' 40-114. Suspected: worn tyres at 4x pace (not yet traced).
- The formation script drives 31% of students' car time in 40-car races (formation lap and safety cars); teachers drive their own.
